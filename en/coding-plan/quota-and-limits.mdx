This page explains how Neosantara Coding Plan calculates Rupiah credit usage, tier allowlists, static model multipliers, free-model daily caps, and Add-on prepaid balances.

## Rupiah Credit Billing

Instead of flat request-turn counts, the backend allocates a monthly **Rupiah credit pool equal to 1.4× your plan price**:

- **Per-request deduction:** completed requests deduct `(PAYG list price of the requested model in IDR) × (static model multiplier)` from your Coding Plan credit windows.
- **Failed requests cost nothing:** requests that fail before completion do not consume credit.
- **PAYG balance isolation:** once any credit window is exhausted, requests hard-stop until the window resets and never charge your standard PAYG deposit balance.

## Spend Windows & Free Trial

Three independent spending windows govern your 30-day cycle anchored to plan activation:

| Window | Credit Share | Reset Behavior |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Rolling 5-Hour** | Max 20% of monthly credit | Sliding 5-hour window (`5-minute` buckets); capacity frees up automatically as older usage expires. |
| **Rolling 7-Day (Weekly)** | Max 50% of monthly credit | Sliding 7-day window (`1-hour` buckets); capacity frees up hourly. |
| **30-Day Cycle** | 100% of monthly credit | Resets cleanly upon renewal or new 30-day activation. |

### In-Flight Concurrency

Concurrency limits cap how many simultaneous in-flight requests your account can execute across subagents and open IDE sessions (`4 / 10 / 20`). Requests above the cap are temporarily rejected until active calls complete.

## Static Model Multipliers

Each covered model deducts credit with a **fixed static multiplier** (multipliers do not scale dynamically with upstream load):

| Multiplier | Group | Example Models |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **1×** | Standard & flagship 1× models | `claude-sonnet-4-6`, `claude-opus-4-6`, `claude-sonnet-5.5`, `claude-opus-5.5`, `gemini-3-flash`, `gemini-3.1-flash-lite`, `gemini-4-hydrogen`, `gemini-4-argon`, `gpt-6-luna`, `garda-core`, `minimax-m2.7`, `gpt-oss-120b`, `mimo-v2.6-flash-free` |
| **1.5×** | Paid-upstream models | `deepseek-v4-flash`, `deepseek-v4.1-flash`, `glm-5.3-flash`, `minimax-m3`, `claude-4.5-sonnet`, `claude-4.5-opus`, `claude-4-5-haiku`, `muse-spark-1.3`, `muse-wonder-1.0`, `muse-ember-1.0`, `ernie-5.5-flash-preview-free`, `mimo-v2.5`, `mimo-v2.5-pro` |

<Note>
  You can inspect the live tier allowlists and multipliers in the Coding Plan dashboard or via `GET /v1/coding/plans`.
</Note>

## Free-Model Daily Request Cap

Zero-cost models (`input = 0`, `output = 0`, such as `ernie-5.5-flash-preview-free` and `mimo-v2.6-flash-free`) do not deduct Rupiah credit, but are capped at **100 requests per UTC day** per user. Responses include `x-neosantara-coding-free-daily-limit` and `x-neosantara-coding-free-daily-remaining` headers.

## Add-on Models & Prepaid Credit

Models designated as **Add-on** (for example `gemini-4-argon` or `claude-fable-5.1` when enabled in the add-on pool) draw from your separate **Prepaid Add-on Balance** rather than plan credit windows. Pricing equals the model's PAYG token cost plus a 40% add-on margin (minimum IDR 100 balance required before dispatch).

| Add-on Top-Up Pack | Amount |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Starter** | IDR 50,000 |
| **Basic** | IDR 100,000 |
| **Standard** | IDR 250,000 |
| **Premium** | IDR 500,000 |

## Credit Optimization Tips

1. **Use Lighter Models for Routine Work**: use free-tier quota models like `mimo-v2.6-flash-free` (up to 100 req/day) or lower-cost models such as `gemini-3.1-flash-lite` or `deepseek-v4.1-flash` for file search and boilerplate edits.
2. **Preserve Prompt Prefixes**: keep system instructions and tool schemas stable so upstream prefix caching cuts input token charges.
3. **Track Active Windows**: monitor your remaining 5-hour, 7-day, and 30-day credit in the [Coding Plan Dashboard](https://app.neosantara.xyz/coding-plan) or `GET /v1/coding/status`.
